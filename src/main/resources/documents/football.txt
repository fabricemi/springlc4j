Football

Football is one of the most popular sports in the world. It is played by two teams of eleven players on a rectangular field. The main objective is to score more goals than the opposing team by kicking the ball into the opponent's goal.

A standard football match lasts 90 minutes, divided into two halves of 45 minutes each, with a break at half-time. The referee ensures that players follow the rules and can give yellow or red cards for fouls. A yellow card is a warning, while a red card means that the player must leave the field.

Players have different positions. The goalkeeper protects the goal. Defenders try to prevent the opposing team from scoring. Midfielders help recover the ball and organize attacks. Forwards mainly try to create scoring opportunities and score goals.

Football has many important competitions, including the FIFA World Cup, the UEFA Champions League, and national leagues. The FIFA World Cup brings together national teams representing their countries and is one of the most watched sporting events in the world.

Beyond competition, football encourages teamwork, discipline, communication, and respect for opponents. It brings millions of fans together and plays an important role in the sporting culture of many countries.
